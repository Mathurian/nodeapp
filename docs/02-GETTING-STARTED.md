# Getting Started

This guide is for general users, contestants, judges, auditors, emcees, tally staff, board members, organizers, and operators who need help entering the app and understanding the basics. It is not a server-installation guide.

## What This Guide Covers

- How to sign in
- What to expect after sign-in
- Where to find help for your role
- Why some results or actions may not be visible
- How to install the app on a phone or tablet
- When to contact your organizer or administrator

## Sign In

1. Open the tenant-specific login page you were given.
2. Enter your email address and password.
3. Complete multi-factor authentication if your organization requires it.
4. If the same email and password are used for more than one tenant-specific account, follow the tenant-selection prompt and continue on the correct tenant login page.

If you cannot sign in, go to [Troubleshooting](10-TROUBLESHOOTING.md).

## Recover Access or Finish an Invitation

- Use the `Forgot password` link from the correct tenant login page if you need a reset link.
- Use the invitation link from your email if your organization requires invite-only registration.
- If you are not sure which tenant login page to use, contact your event organizer or support contact before resetting your password or completing registration.

## What Happens After Sign-In

Your landing page and navigation depend on your role and what your organization has enabled.

- Most users land on the dashboard first.
- Some roles have a dedicated starting area such as board review, emcee tools, or tally work.
- The app may hide items you are not allowed to see yet.

## Role-Based Access Is Normal

Not every user sees the same actions, pages, or results.

- Judges may be able to enter scores without seeing every administrative screen.
- Contestants may only see their own results, and only after publication settings allow it.
- Emcees, auditors, board members, and tally staff each have narrower workflow areas than administrators.
- Organizers and administrators usually see the broadest operational options inside their tenant.

If a page or action is missing, first confirm that you are signed into the correct tenant and role before assuming something is broken.

## Results Visibility

Results are not always visible immediately.

Visibility can depend on:

- your role
- whether scores have been certified
- whether winners or overall results have been published
- event-level release settings or overrides

So if you do not see results yet, that may be expected behavior rather than a bug.

## Using Help

Use the Help area when you need:

- sign-in guidance
- common troubleshooting steps
- role-specific instructions that your organization has chosen to publish

If you are signed in, the Help sidebar may show more content than a public visitor sees.

For scoring and certification help, use
[Scoring and Certification Workflows](15-SCORING-CERTIFICATION-WORKFLOWS.md).

## Install on Mobile as an App (PWA)

You can install Event Manager on many phones and tablets for a faster app-like experience.

### iPhone or iPad (Safari)

1. Open your tenant login or public landing page in Safari.
2. Tap the Share button.
3. Choose `Add to Home Screen`.
4. Confirm the name and add it.

### Android (Chrome or Edge)

1. Open your tenant login or public landing page.
2. Open the browser menu.
3. Choose `Install app`, `Add to Home screen`, or the equivalent browser option.
4. Confirm the installation.

### After Installing

- Open the app from your home screen.
- Sign in normally.
- If the app looks stale after an update, close and reopen it, then refresh once.

## When to Contact Your Organizer or Administrator

Contact your event organizer, administrator, or support contact when:

- you do not have login credentials
- you are in the wrong tenant
- your role appears incorrect
- you expect results or scoring access that never becomes available
- certification or workflow steps appear blocked and the troubleshooting guide does not explain why
